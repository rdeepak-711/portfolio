## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and founders or CTOs at AI-native startups, hiring Forward Deployed and Applied AI engineers. They arrive from a LinkedIn note, an X reply or a job application and give the site a short scan, often on a phone. The job they need done: decide quickly whether Deepak builds real things end to end and checks AI output, then find proof and a way to contact him.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **handmade, curious, careful.**
- A builder's notebook that's fun to flip through, not a corporate résumé.
- Playful on the surface (Dee, the cartoon guide, travels with you), rigorous underneath: every claim is checked, every number sourced.
- Emotional goals: delight in the first ten seconds, then trust. The recruiter should think "this person implements what he learns".

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Reference:** rahulm.me, for its scroll feel. A fixed companion (his paper plane) flies and turns between sections, sections fade up as they arrive, the type is big, and projects alternate. Borrow the feel, not the assets. Dee is our plane.
- **Keep:** the "build log" identity from CLAUDE.md. Warm paper light theme, a single rust accent used rarely, `§ NN —` section markers, hairline rules, mono only for data and labels, left-aligned and asymmetric.
- **Anti-references:**
  - dark mode with neon or glow;
  - purple/blue gradients and gradient text;
  - glassmorphism;
  - identical icon-card grids;
  - centred-everything SaaS heroes;
  - templated dev portfolios.
- **Theme:** light, because people read it on a work break or on a phone in daylight.

### Design Principles
1. **Motion carries meaning.** Dee's path follows the story: brief → build → check → ship. Nothing moves just to move.
2. **Proof never hides behind play.** Every animated moment sits beside a verifiable claim, link or screenshot.
3. **Fast and calm.** Transform and opacity only, no layout animation, no motion library unless it earns its weight. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback: a still Dee, no travel.
4. **Phone first.** The companion and reveals must work at 390px without covering content or the Ask Dee launcher.
5. **One signature, not ten effects.** One memorable scroll companion plus restrained reveals beats scattered micro-animations.
